DaRuVal Talent Program

Overview

DaRuVal Talent Program is a talent attraction, training and professional development program created by DaRuVal, an industrial automation / PLC programming company working on international projects.

The program is aimed primarily at FP students/graduates, technical degree students/graduates and junior profiles interested in industrial automation, PLC programming and international projects.

Core idea

We don't just offer internships. We offer a gateway to an international career.

Supporting concept:

Build your career. See the world.

1. The problem

DaRuVal works on international industrial projects and needs qualified technical people.

Technical knowledge alone is not enough. The ideal candidate also has:

commitment

motivation

curiosity

willingness to learn

adaptability

responsibility

willingness to travel

ability to spend periods away from home

interest in industrial automation

The Talent Program creates a bridge between education and real industrial work.

2. Program structure

01 — FP / CAREER

The candidate is studying an FP or technical degree.

If the educational institution allows it, DaRuVal can establish an internship agreement (convenio de prácticas).

Goal: introduce the candidate to the professional world and to DaRuVal before graduation.

5. Be honest about the job

The work can be demanding.

Participants may:

work long hours during commissioning

spend weeks or months away from home

work in factories

travel internationally

adapt quickly

take responsibility for real industrial systems

Do not market the program as:

“Travel the world while getting paid.”

Instead communicate:

The work can be demanding and you may spend months away from home. In return, you gain real experience, work on real projects, receive paid internship conditions, have flight and accommodation covered while abroad, and get the opportunity to discover places your career takes you to.

The program should self-select people who genuinely want this lifestyle.

21. Golden rule

Every design and copy decision should answer:

If not, reconsider it.

22. Core brand story

DaRuVal Talent Program gives young technical talent the opportunity to move from education into real industrial automation projects. After building their technical foundation in Spain, participants can spend three months working on a real project in the USA, with paid internship conditions and flight and accommodation included while abroad. Along the way, they receive mentoring and access to wellbeing and community initiatives designed to make international projects a positive life experience. If the experience works for both sides, it can become the first step towards joining the DaRuVal team.

Short version:

From FP to the factory floor. From Spain to the USA. DaRuVal Talent Program combines technical training, real industrial projects and international experience in a six-month journey that can become the beginning of your career with DaRuVal.
